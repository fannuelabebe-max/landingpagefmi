Why grocery is different

Grocery loss is its own problem.

Thin margins. Self-checkout at scale. These are grocery’s numbers, from FMI research.

2.1%

Average net profit for food retailers

At that margin, every $1 lost takes about $48 in sales to earn back.

33%

Of supermarket transactions use self-checkout

A third of your front end, with its own loss profile.

67%

Of shoppers say self-checkout lets some customers avoid paying

Your shoppers see the loss too.

85%

Of food retailers report success on shrink reduction goals

At least somewhat successful. APGR is where they compare how.

Sources: FMI, The Food Retailing Industry Speaks (2025 data); FMI, Asset Protection in Food Retail 2026. $48 is 1 ÷ 2.1%.
